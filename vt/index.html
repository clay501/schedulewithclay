Therapy in Vermont

At NEK Family Counseling Center, Clay sees clients virtually throughout Vermont, under the supervision of Rachael Little, LMFT. Sessions are virtual, so you can meet from home, work, or wherever you feel most comfortable, as long as you're in Vermont (and not driving) at the time of your session.

Who Clay works with
Individuals ages 14+ and, in particular, LGBTQ+ and neurodivergent people, members of other historically marginalized communities, and anyone looking for a space to explore, learn, and heal.
Approach
Psychodynamic, person-centered, existential, narrative, and DBT-informed approaches. Whatever you bring into the room, Clay will accept you without judgment, and you'll work to make sense of it all together.
Why Vermont?
Clay fell in love with Vermont over 30 years ago during his first visit to the state with his family. Since then, his appreciation for and ties to Vermont have grown deeper, and he cherishes the time he's spent in Chittenden and Lamoille counties. Clay's admiration and reverence for the Northeast Kingdom is captured in part in Robert Frost's “A Servant to Servants.”
